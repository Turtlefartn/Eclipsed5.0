R&D & Future Features

The workbench page for Eclipsed5.0: what is being researched and what is planned next. Everything here is a work in progress — that is the point of writing it down in public.

Research & Development

Active research lines across the Journey to the End of the Universe:

  • Orca keyboard — tactile sensors on the tips of gloved thumbs, connected to an interface orcas and dolphins can actually play. Two-way communication, not trained tricks.
  • Cetacean gear — blowhole scuba rigs (including a liquid-breathing mode) and a thought-reading collar, developed with engineers, neuroscientists, marine biologists, and coders.
  • Mass bioluminescence — engineered bioluminescent giant kelp, glow concentrated in the float bladders, as living lighting for ocean settlements.
  • Cleaner organisms — cataloguing nature's cleaners (cleaner wrasse, doctor fish) and designing organisms that clean humans.
  • CO2 Balls — captured carbon as a storable, shippable, phase-agnostic dispersal agent. Greenhouse enrichment dosing is the strongest near-term use; dry-ice balls are the flagship form.
  • Time-out Balls — a throwable traction-denial dispersal system. Food-grade tasty payload class for the wrong-person case, expanding foam for ground and barriers only, and a sulfur stink class kept deliberately separate for marking and flushing.
  • Rock crushing — reseeding rivers and lakes with crushed basalt (quarry fines first) to replace the mineral conveyor that dams broke; doubles as aquatic enhanced rock weathering.
  • HEAR-TS — the hydrogen trimaran work boats and capsule fleet that service the Project 84 anchors.
  • Emojis that last forever — a symbol set durable enough to carry the message across deep time, readable long after every current language is gone.
  • Hurricane suppression research — tracked on its own page; situational tools stay in the arsenal at geoengineering scale.

Future Features

On the roadmap, in roughly the order the ladder climbs:

  • One Terms of Service — a single terms-of-service page for the whole site, replacing the per-page patchwork.
  • Custom domain — Eclipsed5.0 on its own domain, off the github.io address.
  • Starfleet Academy — the capstone school of the off-world ladder; Plan #42's 260 schools are its feeder system.
  • 24 timezone fleets — a HEAR-TS fleet for every timezone on Earth.
  • Rocket races — a solar system grand prix: full circuit past every planet, fastest lap wins.
  • Floating skyports — movable airborne pickup hubs where small VTOL craft meet long-haul airships. No ground airports needed.
  • D-Mom 6.0 — the Mars step: the Moon as shipyard and gas station, seed cities thrown Moon-to-Mars whole.
  • Project 1024 — wiring Mars's iron core as an antenna.
  • Project 512 — the Pluto ejection observatory.
  • Project 24 — metallic hydrogen superconductors from a stripped Jupiter.

The ladder runs Earth → Luna → Jupiter → Alpha Centauri over ~28,000 years. This page is the bottom rung's notebook.
