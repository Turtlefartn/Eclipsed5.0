# When The Irish

When Irish Eyes are smiling,

They're usually up to something...!

Food missed connection,

Captain's called wrong?

Vinegar, fried, floor, chicken.

Rogers Roth-es-burgered.

Lost numbers, missed? Messages...

Taken away, play-girl-eyez'd

The devil, playing the fiddle.

A demon with no voice.

Delt-a-choice? Banjo noise.

What's the price....

Acknowledged the evils

of alcohol? Striving to

eliminate the wine cellar!

One bottle at a time!
