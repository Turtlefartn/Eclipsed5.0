## What a text pull can't carry

- **Embed-only pages** — Licks & Kicks, Chose Wisely, PRIMED, Smackdown!, Chose Yo Own Adventure, and Bad People Do Bad Things hold their content in Google Sites embeds (Drive files etc.). Those files still live in Google Drive; the pages here are stubs pointing back to the live pages.
- **Images** — page images (e.g. the 10 on Drag'n Knights, shots on P04N & CKN and Lost Coast, section banners) are marked as placeholders in position; the image files themselves weren't extracted.
- **Dead links** — the Pen'Dragg'n page links out to muse.ai/s/ pages that were deleted 2026-10-13; the links are preserved as written and flagged in that file.
