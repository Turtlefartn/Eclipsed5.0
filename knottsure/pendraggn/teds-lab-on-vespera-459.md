# Ted's Lab on Vespera #459

Year: Unknown; Location: Vespera - Underground Lab.

Timmy #? enters The Lab.

Fuuuuuckkkk! What happened! Timmy checks the security footage and sees: Thomas enters The Lab.

Wait... what the fuck are these... where are my pods, and where did all these come from? He goes to Tammy J's pod and knocks on the glass. When her black eyes open, she sees Thomas and bursts through the pod... and yells FOR PRIME! and snaps his neck, both falling to the floor as she 'powers' down.

Timmy disposes of Thomas and places Tammy J back in her pod and says, "That's why you're my favorite lil sis, but you're not ready yet," and resumes the bioshell process.

Timmy, worried that Thomas 13 and 14 might find the new batch, loads the rest of the pods onto a ship, and returns to Earth to hide them through time.

Ted 'enters' the lab, sees 26 brand-new vats, all female drones, empty shells. Labeled Tammy A - Z. The first one is open, with the unit on the ground.

"Idiot, didn't load the base firmware"

As soon as he says this, Tammy J snaps his arm in two and uses it to stab him in the chest and bolts out of the lab, laughing hysterically. Ted, in shock, has no clue how this drone is operating; it should just be an empty shell. The L.I.F.E. module wasn't installed... What has Thomas done? Or was it Timmy... no notes this time... collapses.

Tammy J has made it to Ted's ship and started her journey to cover up and fix Ted's mistakes.

"Idiot... "Carry the '2'" 'Four-get-Four' Duty... The mission.
