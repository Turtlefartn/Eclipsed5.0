03 / OUTPUTS

What six months buys.

Seven decision-grade work products, designed to resolve scope and price the build before major capital is committed.

01

Ledger architecture

Issuer roles, verification model, record structure, permissions and lifecycle.

02

Scoring model specification

Additive logic, upgrade paths, governance, validation and anti-gaming controls.

03

Plankton economy design

Good deeds earn Plankton badges; shared access replaces unnecessary ownership through durable, repairable resources.

04

Civic legal framework

Defined scope for driving, gun responsibility, required training and social permissions.

05

Pilot operating plans

Costed, sequenced plans for Prime 42 and Omega42.

06

New Eden blueprint

Operating integration for education, civic participation and shared resources.

07

Full build-phase budget

Staffing, stages, dependencies, risks and capital requirements for execution.

05 / DE-RISKING

Prove the model before human rollout.

Two focused pilot programs establish operating evidence quickly, then feed lessons into the human system design.

Primate pilot

Prime 42

Monkeys and apes test coordinated systems, learning loops and resource sharing in a faster, stronger population.

Marine pilot

Omega42

Orcas and dolphins test verified learning, cooperation and shared-resource systems in a faster, stronger population.
