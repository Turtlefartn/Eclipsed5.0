Uplift Project / Working definition

“Uplifting is raising the cognitive abilities of ‘lesser’ species.”
existing mind expanded mind

The founder’s method

That’s how you uplift — take dumbest and smartest and get them to talk.

Pair the extremes within a species and establish real communication between them. The smartest pull the dumbest up through talk itself: capability spreads mind to mind, rather than arriving as an instruction imposed from above.

The standing obligation

Uplift must serve the uplifted, not just the uplifters — raising a mind creates obligations to that mind.

Measure of success Not what the new mind can do for us, but whether its greater capability enlarges its own life.
Priority order
  1. Talking — establish shared language and genuine back-and-forth.
  2. Training — only after they can talk back.
Cetacean work Apply the same method to dolphins and orcas: connect the most and least capable communicators, with conversation as the goal and training second.
