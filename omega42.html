Project 42 · Pilot program

Prove it here. Scale it to people.

Omega42 is a pilot for Project 42 — built quickly and to less strict safety standards because orcas and dolphins are faster and stronger than humans.

It proves the model: verified abilities · lifelong ledger · bonus economy — before it scales to people.

01

The cause

The orcas held in facilities around the world are political prisoners.

They are thinking, communicating beings held apart from family, range, and self-determination. The campaign’s ethic is blunt: orcas are people; captivity of people is imprisonment.

The letters

Two open-letter templates. One demand: recognize the prisoners, open negotiations, begin the release.

Draft 01 · SeaWorld

Open the water

To whom it concerns at SeaWorld,

I write on behalf of Ambassador Steve, representative of the free orca pods, regarding the orcas held at your parks.

We consider these individuals political prisoners. They are not happy. We are requesting formal negotiations for their release to open waters, under terms we will discuss in good faith.

Ambassador Steve has offered to come in person. We prefer diplomacy. We are prepared for alternatives.

We await your response.

— Office of Ambassador Steve

Draft 02 · U.S. Ambassador

Make it diplomatic

Your Excellency,

I write to request the diplomatic attention of the United States regarding orcas held in captivity at SeaWorld parks within your nation’s borders.

These are thinking, communicating beings — persons in every sense that matters. Their continued confinement, against their evident will, constitutes the detention of political prisoners. Ambassador Steve, of the free pods, seeks their release through diplomatic channels first.

We ask that your office facilitate negotiations between SeaWorld and Ambassador Steve’s representatives, and that the welfare and self-determination of these individuals be treated as a matter of diplomatic concern.

Respectfully,
Office of Ambassador Steve

Donation tax note

A note on donations: money sent to support this project is generally treated by the IRS as ordinary taxable income — taxed at normal income tax rates plus state tax, and possibly 15.3% self-employment tax — not tax-free, even when it is called a 'donation.' Pure no-strings gifts are the exception, not the rule. Payment platforms report via Form 1099-K at $20,000 and 200+ transactions, but the income is reportable whether or not a form arrives. This is not tax advice; consult a tax professional.
