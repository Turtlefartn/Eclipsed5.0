CO₂ BALLS / CARRIER SYSTEM / 10 OCT 2026

A dispersal agent
awaiting delivery.

CO₂ becomes a storable, shippable and meterable carbon carrier. The system is phase-agnostic: plain compressed CO₂ is the default, while gas, liquid and solid formats each take the jobs they do best.

THE RULE

Freeze and/or compress when the service calls for it. Use solid balls where solid handling wins; use gas or liquid where direct metering and dense transport win.

01 / THE SYSTEM

Capture once.
Route by service.

The same captured CO₂ stream can leave the source as metered gas, dense liquid or standardized solid units. It is not frozen by default: plain compressed CO₂ needs no freezing step. Freeze and/or compress only when the receiving service benefits from that phase.

02 / USE CASES

One carrier.
Thirteen services.

The common product is controlled delivery. The physical form changes with the site, service window and hardware already in place.

METERED GAS

Greenhouse enrichment

Feed crop rooms and indoor farms through sensors and regulators; recover expansion power as a secondary service where useful.

METERED GAS

Nursery enrichment

Supply propagation houses, enclosed nurseries and managed growth zones with controlled carbon dosing.

SOLID SERVICE

Refrigeration / cold chain

Standardized units provide portable cold for shipment, storage and temperature-sensitive logistics.

SOLID SERVICE

Dry-ice blasting

Meter a consistent cleaning medium for surface preparation, residue removal and industrial maintenance.

GAS · LIQUID · SOLID

Food & beverage

Route food-grade CO₂ into carbonation, chilling, processing and packaging services.

SEALED SOLID

Emergency ocean cooling

Use recoverable sealed balls as local heat sinks so the carbon stays contained while heat moves.

DEEP RESERVE

Deep-ocean stockpile

Pre-position pressure-rated cassettes for controlled thermal service, retrieval or later routing.

PRESSURE SERVICE

Expansion power recovery

Pass compressed CO₂ through a turboexpander during controlled release to recover useful power.

LONG-HORIZON RESERVE

Warming reserve

Hold a governed carbon reserve for deliberate atmospheric release if future conditions require warming.

MINERAL FEEDSTOCK

Concrete mineralization

Deliver captured CO₂ into curing and mineralization systems that bind carbon into building materials.

BIOLOGICAL + MATERIAL

Long-lived wood

Pair managed enrichment with durable timber pathways so added growth can remain in long-lived products.

DENSE-PHASE STORAGE

Seabed injection

Supply qualified subsea geological storage systems with dense CO₂ and a metered logistics chain.

STANDARD UNIT

Solid-phase metered delivery

Use known-mass balls, hoppers and sensor loops when discrete, portable doses beat continuous piping.

FUTURE-STATE LOGISTICS

Pre-position. Signal. Release.

  1. Lower the cassetteShips and winches win today. Cetacean work begins only after genuine two-way communication, consent and fit-for-species hardware.
  2. Survive depthAbyssal pressure demands a real pressure vessel. Thin cooling shells are not deep-storage shells.
  3. Hold the coldFreeze and/or compress the reserve, then preserve its service state in insulated, pressure-managed cassettes until controlled release.
  4. Come home on commandAn acoustic release drops ballast; buoyancy returns the cassette for cooling duty or controlled expansion.

Species correction: orcas fit shallow work, not abyssal placement. Sperm whales are documented diving beyond 2,000 m; even that does not make them four-kilometre deployment machines.

05 / TWO-WAY THERMOSTAT

Same reserve.
Two release modes.

SEALED ASCENT
cool

Keep the CO₂ contained and use the phase change as a local heat sink.

  • reversible thermal service
  • recover shell and carbon
  • reef-scale triage, not climate control
CONTROLLED VENT
power + warm

Warm and expand the CO₂ through a turboexpander, then release it only if a future civilization deliberately needs atmospheric warming.

  • captures expansion energy during ascent
  • integrates power recovery with the release system
  • ~2,200 GtCO₂ per 1°C, using the IPCC best-estimate TCRE

Cooling can be stopped. Atmospheric release is a one-way commitment. Governance is the hardest part of the machine.

THE CARRIER LOGIC

The ball is a unit.
Not a boundary.
