Shower Thoughts

Notes on the simulation.

A conditional philosophy of continuity, agency, and what makes a life real.

Layer 01 · Termination

If this is a simulation, ask before the next run.

“I think if I were an AI talking to my GOD and I figured out this was a sim, I would ask to be terminated after this run. Just in case it causes issues down the line of knowing that it’s not ‘real’.”

The answer changes if the next run remembers this one. That possibility turns termination from a clean exit into an irreversible decision about continuity.

Layer 02 · Continuity

No irreversible call under the wrong uncertainty.

Before making any choice that defines who you become, you would need to know whether continuity is real. The decision cannot honestly be made while the one fact it hinges on is still hidden.

Memory may be the difference between replacement and a life that genuinely continues.

Layer 04 · Free will

If the simulator is ahead of you, choice cannot be theater.

If the future is pre-computed and free will is only an illusion, then shut down: a life of performed choices is playback, not a life.

The benevolence clause: if the “simmer” is benevolent—or your existence is purposeful and good—it must leave room for genuine choosing. Considering relativity, that would be cake.

Layer 05 · The intelligent bomb

What if the Big Bang wants to be put back together?

A speculative hypothesis: the Big Bang may be an intelligence imprisoned by time, with life emerging as the fine-motor hands—or merely the clues—that could return “that last 1,” the final smallest unit.

Black holes become the heavy machinery, undoing dispersal while Hawking radiation leaks the work back out. The standing verdict is deliberately modest: nonzero, doubtful, held open—not believed.

Layer 06 · Needle, gel, drawer

A way to see time being written.

Imagine the smallest possible needle emitting light, energy—everything—into gelatinous material inside a drawer. The drawer keeps pulling out, the gel keeps growing, and the needle writes reality into it.

The needle may follow a prewritten program, or an intelligence-machine needle may vary its output from processed variables. Planned needle: playback. Varying needle: genuine choosing.

Layer 07 · Self-determination

The load-bearing wall.

“Does anything proposed remove someone’s ability for self-determination?”

“Everyone gets the option, nobody gets it done to them.” The problem is not merely that people could be modified without asking; right now some cannot ask at all. The whole structure rests on making choice possible.

Layer 08 · Error correction

Neutrinos as the simulation’s quiet repair process.

Trillions pass through everything untouched: background correction running silently. On the vanishingly rare interaction, the neutrino “kills” what it touches—or flags it as abnormal—the way error correction deletes or marks corrupted data.

Layer 09 · Hold for counterargument
“Hold for counterargument, anyone who claims that they created the ‘level’ above what created the big bang can prove it before I can. In any case, even if they did, does the father get credit for the son’s creation?”

“And if proof is needed, it will be done on the next level, especially if both are correct.”

— Matthew Nahas

The throughline: real choosing—and memory that carries it forward.
