legal reality · strategy memo · 5 oct 2026

You can’t claim it.

The sea and Antarctica are built to reject conquest. The move is not to own them. The move is to become the institution trusted to run what everyone needs.

The law says no.

Not “difficult.” Not “find a clever flag.” A unilateral sovereignty claim is the wrong instrument in both places.

SEA / UNCLOS

Open ocean is not available.

Article 89 says no state may subject any part of the high seas to its sovereignty. The deep seabed beyond national jurisdiction is the common heritage of humankind, administered through an international regime.

No title by declaration
ANTARCTICA / 1959 TREATY

The claims are frozen.

Article IV preserves everyone’s old position without deciding who is right. While the treaty is in force, activity on the continent creates no sovereignty rights, and no new or enlarged territorial claim may be asserted.

No title by occupation

Know which ocean you mean.

“The sea” is four different legal spaces. Ownership, resource rights, navigation and seabed rights do not travel together.

0–12 nm

Territorial sea. Coastal-state sovereignty, subject to rules including innocent passage.

to 24 nm

Contiguous zone. Limited enforcement powers; not a second belt of full sovereignty.

to 200 nm

EEZ. The coastal state gets sovereign rights over resources and specified jurisdiction. It does not own the water as territory.

beyond

High seas + the Area. Navigation remains open; sovereignty claims fail. Deep-seabed resources sit under the common-heritage regime.

THE FLOATING-ISLAND LOOPHOLE IS NOT A LOOPHOLE.

An artificial island does not become an island in the legal sense. It generates no territorial sea and no exclusive economic zone. A platform can be owned and operated; the ocean around it does not become a country.

THE “2048 MINING OPENS” STORY IS WRONG.

The Antarctic environmental protocol does not expire in 2048. From that point a review conference may be requested. The mineral ban continues unless a binding legal regime meeting the protocol’s conditions is actually in force.

The real playbook.

If the objective is durable control over environmental outcomes, replace “claim” with a ladder of consent, usefulness and jurisdiction.

Name a function, not a kingdom.

Frame the body as a global-commons trust, environmental authority or treaty organization. Its jurisdiction begins with a defined job: protection, science, rescue, monitoring or logistics.

Become useful before asking to become supreme.

Operate lawful projects that states already need: ocean observation, cleanup, habitat protection, scientific transport, emergency response and transparent environmental data.

Get sponsored by states.

Ships need flags. Projects inside an EEZ need coastal-state consent. Antarctic work runs through treaty parties and domestic permit systems. Build a coalition that can carry the institution into the rooms where law is made.

Turn practice into an agreement.

Negotiate delegated authority: inspection, standards, protected-area management, data custody, fees and dispute procedures. This is the moment operations become jurisdiction.

Fund services—not fantasy collateral.

Use member contributions, service fees, trade, grants and treaty-authorized revenue. Future lawful resource revenue can be contingent income. Unowned Antarctic or deep-seabed resources cannot honestly be pledged as owned collateral.

Make exit harder than entry.

Publish every decision and every unit of revenue. Give members inspection rights, due process and a reason to keep using the system. Durable legitimacy is the closest thing the commons has to title.

Project 84: legal stress test.

The constitutional language can stay ambitious. These are the parts that need translation before the outside world can sign anything.

Seven sea chairs
Reframe

Representation is viable. Ownership is not. Write the chairs as trustees of a domain, with powers created by the charter and later treaties.

Antarctic + undersea resources back debt
Blocked

Project 84 does not own those assets. Replace the collateral claim with a contingent right to future revenue lawfully assigned by treaty, concession or benefit-sharing regime.

World tax
Needs treaty

Call it a member assessment until states ratify a binding levy and implement collection through their own law.

Donated military assets
Narrow it

Assets can serve consent-based environmental enforcement, rescue and inspection. In Antarctica, military measures are prohibited; military personnel or equipment are allowed only for science or another peaceful purpose.

Authority above leaders in I.P.A. (International Protection Agency) matters
Delegate it

That rank exists only where states explicitly hand over competence. Define the subject matter, enforcement boundary, appeal path and withdrawal rule.

The win is not a deed. It is a recognized mandate, ships that answer the call, institutions that accept the rulings, and a public that treats Project 84 as the planet’s environmental operator. Own the job long enough and the title matters less.

Still open.

The law guide closes the sovereignty question. The Project 84 constitution still has six live decisions.

Article XIII
Choose

Set the amendment threshold: five of seven seats or a defined supermajority.

Ratification
Blank

Name the date, who signs, what count makes it valid and when it enters into force.

Emergency powers
Blank

The “OH FUCK” duty exists, but no one is yet authorized to act in the first hours or checked afterward.

Citizenship oath
Unwritten

Citizenship requires an oath. The oath itself still needs exact words.
