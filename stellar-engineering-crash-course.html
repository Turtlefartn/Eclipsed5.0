Stellar Engineering Crash Course

≈ 15 FOCUSED HOURS

Ten physical ideas, one numerical meta-skill, and the anchors needed to reason at stellar scale.

01 / MISSION

Why this exists

Relocate the whole solar system with a stellar engine—toward Alpha Centauri, or into rendezvous with inbound Barnard’s Star—while carrying an ignited, higher-mass Jupiter as the second lamp.

Then learn to aim solar flares for planetary defense, including the problem of parking next to flare-star Proxima.

stellar engineering Kardashev Type II
02 / COURSE

The modules

  1. 1. Vector calculus, the intuitions. div = does stuff flow outward, curl = does it swirl, grad = which way is steepest. ~2 hrs. You need these to read everything below.

  2. 2. Maxwell’s four, the stories. Skip the proofs. The one that matters: ∇·B = 0—field lines can’t end, which is exactly why they can only “break” by reconnection.

  3. 3. Field energy: B²/2μ₀. Magnetic pressure. The single most-used number in these projects—it’s how you price any magnetic idea in joules.

  4. 4. Frozen-in flux. In a plasma, field lines move with the fluid. You can’t grab one without grabbing the ocean it’s frozen into. This is why steering is hard.

  5. 5. Alfvén speed and plasma beta. vA = B/√(μ₀ρ): how fast magnetic news travels. β = gas pressure ÷ magnetic pressure: who wins, the gas or the field. Two numbers that tell you which regime you’re in.

  6. 6. Reconnection. Squeeze oppositely-directed lines together → they snap, rejoin, dump energy as heat and motion. Slow version and fast version. This is flares. This is the aiming lever.

  7. 7. Hydrostatic equilibrium. dP/dr = −ρg. Squeeze a ball of gas and the middle gets hot. The entire Jupiter answer: mass, not matches.

  8. 8. Lawson criterion. Fusion needs temperature × density × confinement time, all at once. Why you can’t spark a gas giant.

  9. 9. Orbital mechanics, one equation. vis-viva: v² = GM(2/r − 1/a). Moving mass around stars—star-lifting logistics, parking a Shkadov mirror.

  10. 10. Radiation pressure. F = L/c. The entire Shkadov thruster in four characters.

  11. Meta-skill: numerical simulation. Nobody solves real MHD by hand; the field runs on codes.

04 / 2026-10-04

Key results

  1. Field lines can’t be “snapped”—but reconnection is the real equivalent, and the Sun does it constantly.
  2. Moving the Sun: easy physics, impossible engineering. Aiming flares: possible engineering, hostile physics.
  3. Planets ride along for free under gentle thrust: push ≪ orbital binding.
  4. Siphoning plasma can’t ignite Jupiter—heat alone isn’t enough. Sustained fusion needs temperature, density, and confinement; making Jupiter star-like also requires substantially more mass. That’s star lifting, and the numbers don’t close via solar wind.
