The mission (why this exists)

Relocate the whole solar system with a stellar engine — toward Alpha Centauri, or rendezvous with inbound Barnard's Star — carrying an ignited, higher-mass Jupiter as the second lamp. Plus: aim solar flares (planetary defense, e.g. parking next to flare-star Proxima). Field: stellar engineering, Kardashev Type II.

Possibly using Jupiter's Metallic Hydrogen core as a superconductor to open wormholes and traverse the universe in a controlled FTL fashion.

The modules

  • 1. Vector calculus, the intuitions. div = does stuff flow outward, curl = does it swirl, grad = which way is steepest. ~2 hrs. You need these to read everything below.
  • 2. Maxwell's four, the stories. Skip the proofs. The one that matters: ∇·B = 0 — field lines can't end, which is exactly why they can only "break" by reconnection.
  • 3. Field energy: B²/2μ₀. Magnetic pressure. The single most-used number in these projects — it's how you price any magnetic idea in joules.
  • 4. Frozen-in flux. In a plasma, field lines move with the fluid. You can't grab one without grabbing the ocean it's frozen into. This is why steering is hard.
  • 5. Alfvén speed and plasma beta. v_A = B/√(μ₀ρ): how fast magnetic news travels. β = gas pressure ÷ magnetic pressure: who wins, the gas or the field. Two numbers that tell you which regime you're in.
  • 6. Reconnection. Squeeze oppositely-directed lines together → they snap, rejoin, dump energy as heat and motion. Slow version and fast version. This is flares. This is the aiming lever.
  • 7. Hydrostatic equilibrium. dP/dr = −ρg. Squeeze a ball of gas and the middle gets hot. The entire Jupiter answer: mass, not matches.
  • 8. Lawson criterion. Fusion needs temperature × density × confinement time, all at once. Why you can't spark a gas giant.
  • 9. Orbital mechanics, one equation. vis-viva: v² = GM(2/r − 1/a). Moving mass around stars — star-lifting logistics, parking a Shkadov mirror.
  • 10. Radiation pressure. F = L/c. The entire Shkadov thruster in four characters.
  • Meta-skill: numerical simulation. Nobody solves real MHD by hand; the field runs on codes.

Anchors to memorize

  • Sun: 2×10³⁰ kg, 3.8×10²⁶ W
  • Jupiter: 1.9×10²⁷ kg
  • Fusion needs 0.075 solar masses (13 Mj for deuterium)
  • Big flare: 10²⁵ J (~50M Tsar Bombas, ~15,000 yrs of human energy)
  • 1 AU = 1.5×10¹¹ m
  • Caplan thruster: 10⁻⁹ m/s², ~50 ly/Myr → Alpha Centauri (4.37 ly) in ~90k yrs
  • Shkadov (passive): 10⁻¹² m/s² — far too slow for relocation
  • Barnard's Star: inbound, ~3.8 ly closest approach in ~12,000 yrs

Key results from the 2026-10-04 session

  • Field lines can't be "snapped" — but reconnection is the real equivalent, and the Sun does it constantly.
  • Moving the Sun: easy physics, impossible engineering. Aiming flares: possible engineering, hostile physics.
  • Planets ride along for free under gentle thrust (push ≪ orbital binding).
  • Siphoning plasma can't ignite Jupiter — fusion needs mass/compression, not heat. That's star lifting, and the numbers don't close via solar wind.
  • Aim flares at what and full mission design still open.
