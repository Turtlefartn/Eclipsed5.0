Read the estimates carefully

“Under 30” means ages 0–29. The headline population counts sum six five-year age groups from the UN WPP 2024 dataset mirrored by Our World in Data. North America includes Northern America, Central America and the Caribbean.

  • 2050 under-30: under-25 is the UN medium-variant projection; ages 25–29 are modeled from cohort survival and birth trends.
  • Urban/rural: a total-population proxy. A continent-wide youth-specific breakdown was not found.
  • Sex split: modeled from sex ratio at birth with an adjustment for excess male mortality before age 30.
  • Labour and connectivity: youth means ages 15–24. ILO and ITU regions do not map perfectly to continents, so several values are blended or approximate.
  • Oceania literacy: not reported as a continent aggregate by UIS/WB; the near-universal figure is explicitly an assumption, not a measured regional value.
  • SolidStructured dataset or published table.
  • BlendCalculated from published subregional values.
  • ModelDemographic estimate with stated assumptions.
  • ApproxProxy or approximate chart reading.
